Sub: Affiliated Colleges - Grant of extension of Provisional affiliation to DVM College of Business Management, Charlapally to offer the course(s) as applied for - Orders - Issued.

Based on the approval of the AICTE and permission from the Government of Telangana and on the recommendations of the University Inspection Committee, sanction is hereby accorded for grant of extension of provisional affiliation to D.V.M. College of Business Management, Charlapally Village & Post, Nalgonda (M), Nalgonda District, to offer MBA Course with an intake of (54) for the Academic year 2024-25. However, the College has to fulfill the following conditions/ deficiencies.

CONDITIONS:
  1. One Professor and Two Associate Professors are to be appointed.
  2. Library books are inadequate and books worth of Rs 10,000/- are to be procured.
  3. College has to ensure that the faculty cadre ratio of 1:2:5 (i.e. One Professor, Two Associate Professors and Five lecturers) and faculty, student ratio of 1:15 is to be maintained on the approved intake at any time.
  4. The Compliance report with relevant evidences must be forwarded through University nominee on the Governing Body and to be submitted in time to the University.
  5. The details of approved faculty members along with their photographs are to be uploaded in the University website www.mguniversitty.in and hard copy to be submitted to the University.
  6. The faculty members are to be deputed for Refresher course/Orientation programmes / Faculty Development programmes conducted by Mahatma Gandhi University / Other Universities / Other Institutions.
  7. Student Seminars are to be conducted in every week. A copy of the proceedings of such seminars need to be sent at the end of each semester to the concerned Head of the Department, MGU.
  8. Teaching diaries and student attendance registers are to be maintained properly.
  9. Acquaintance register is to be maintained properly.

The Compliance report on the fulfillment of deficiencies /conditions pointed out by the Inspection Committee along with the documentary evidence is submitted within one week in the following proforma, through the University representative on the Governing Body of the College.
